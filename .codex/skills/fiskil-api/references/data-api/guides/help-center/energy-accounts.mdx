# Energy - Business Accounts (/data-api/guides/help-center/energy-accounts)

Guidance and resources for connecting business energy accounts and nominated representatives.



Connecting Energy Business Accounts and Nominating Representatives [#connecting-energy-business-accounts-and-nominating-representatives]

SME and C\&I energy retailer accounts can have a variety of users such as payroll, staff and administrators. To share data you will need to grant specific users the access privileges to share data on behalf of the business.

This is called a Nominated Representative. Once a Nominated Representative is set up, any user with the appropriate privileges can share access to your business's data with any third party application. This guide outlines the processes for adding nominated representatives with major Australian energy providers.

These nomination processes are determined by each energy provider under CDR regulations. Fiskil cannot bypass these requirements, but we're here to support you throughout the process.

Origin Energy [#origin-energy]

Online [#online]

Contact [businesscustomers@originenergy.com.au](mailto:businesscustomers@originenergy.com.au) with:

* Letter of Authority
* Account Number

Small to Medium Businesses [#small-to-medium-businesses]

Call `1300 661 544` to add an authorised representative for CDR.

Large Commercial and Industrial (C&I) Businesses [#large-commercial-and-industrial-ci-businesses]

Call `13 23 34` to add an authorised representative for CDR.

After Setup [#after-setup]

Once added, manage via the online dashboard:

[dashboard.mydata.cdr.originenergy.com.au](https://dashboard.mydata.cdr.originenergy.com.au)

Energy Australia [#energy-australia]

All Business Accounts [#all-business-accounts]

Call `1800 146 749` to nominate a CDR Representative as a contact on the business account.

To Prepare [#to-prepare]

Ensure you have:

* NMI
* ABN
* Account number

After Setup [#after-setup-1]

Identity will be verified via a one-time passcode during first-time setup.

Use EA's online CDR Customer Dashboard (via My Account) to complete the consent and sharing flow, and for ongoing management.

AGL [#agl]

Nominated Representative Requirements [#nominated-representative-requirements]

The nominated person must:

* Have an email registered and authenticated by AGL.
* Be at least 18 years old.

How to Nominate [#how-to-nominate]

The Primary Account Holder must contact your AGL Account Manager or Customer Service to set up an authorised person as a Nominated Representative for CDR.

In some cases, this will first require adding them as an authorised contact.

After Setup [#after-setup-2]

Manage data sharing arrangements for the business via:

* The AGL app
* My Account page
* AGL's CDR dashboard

Add an authorised person to the account via My Account or by contacting AGL directly.

Contact AGL [#contact-agl]

Customer Service: `131 245`

Small Business Accounts [#small-business-accounts]

Call `13 39 08`

Large Business Accounts [#large-business-accounts]

Email your request to [ci.services@alintaenergy.com.au](mailto:ci.services@alintaenergy.com.au)

Or contact your dedicated Account Manager.

To Prepare [#to-prepare-1]

Ensure you have:

* Business verification details

Note: Only the primary account holder can complete this process.

After Setup [#after-setup-3]

Manage consents via Alinta's CDR dashboard (you will receive a one-time passcode for verification).

Ergon Energy [#ergon-energy]

How to Nominate [#how-to-nominate-1]

To add an authorised representative for a business account, complete the "Appointment of authorised representative - business form".

Submit it via:

* Post: Ergon Energy Retail, PO Box 308, Rockhampton QLD 4700
* Email: [enquiry@support.ergonretail.com.au](mailto:enquiry@support.ergonretail.com.au)

Note: Be sure to include your account details and ensure the form is signed by an authorised person within your business.

Alternative Option [#alternative-option]

Call the Business Customer Service Line: `1300 135 210`

Red Energy [#red-energy]

How to Nominate [#how-to-nominate-2]

Only the Company Director or Company Secretary of the business can nominate a representative for CDR data sharing.

Contact Red Energy to appoint a CDR Nominated Representative.

Call `131 806` (Australia) or `+613 9977 6000`.

To Prepare [#to-prepare-3]

Before data sharing can begin, the Nominated Representative individual must be added as an authorised person on the business account. A pre-existing authorisation for an individual to transact on the account does not extend to CDR services.

Ensure the nominated person's full details are ready.

After Setup [#after-setup-4]

Manage consents via Red Energy's CDR Dashboard.

If You're Not Satisfied [#if-youre-not-satisfied]

If you experience difficulty or delays with a provider during the nomination or consent process, you can:

* Raise a complaint with the provider directly - each provider has an internal complaints process.
* If unresolved, escalate the matter to the ACCC at [ACCC-CDR@accc.gov.au](mailto:ACCC-CDR@accc.gov.au) or via the CDR Service Management Portal.
* Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if the issue relates to privacy or access rights under CDR.

For additional assistance or to discuss your concerns, contact Fiskil Support at [support@fiskil.com](mailto:support@fiskil.com) - we're happy to help guide you through your options.
