# Banking - Business Accounts (/data-api/guides/help-center/business-accounts)

Guidance and resources for sharing data from business banking accounts.



Sharing data from Business Accounts [#sharing-data-from-business-accounts]

Under the Consumer Data Right (CDR) Open Banking, some banks require businesses and other non-individual account holders (e.g., trusts) to appoint a Nominated Representative before they can share data.

A Nominated Representative (also known as a delegate) is an individual within an organisation who is authorised to manage and share CDR data on its behalf. The process for appointing a Nominated Representative varies by bank - some offer online registration, others provide in-app options, and some require a paper or digital form submission.

Business bank accounts can have a variety of users such as payroll, staff and administrators. To share data via Open Banking you will need to grant specific users the access privileges to share data on behalf of the business. Once this is set up any user with the appropriate privileges can share access to your businesses data with any accredited third party application. This section provides a list of available guides on how to set up business sharing with each respective institution.
